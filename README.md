## 💡 What is SuggestMe AI?

**SuggestMe AI** is an intelligent companion designed for everyday local choices. Instead of endless scrolling across maps, social feeds, and review sites, SuggestMe provides context-aware, personalized recommendations based on your intent, mood, location, and lifestyle.

Whether finding local events, discovering community activities, exploring neighborhood spots, or deciding what to do next, SuggestMe delivers clear, actionable suggestions in seconds.

---

## ⚡ Core Highlights

* **📍 Real-Time Local Discovery:** Instant visibility into neighborhood activities, alerts, experiences, and open gatherings.
* **🧠 Context-Aware Recommendations:** Tailors suggestions to time of day, weather, intent, and personal lifestyle preferences.
* **⚡ Zero Friction:** Cut through choice paralysis with instant, curated options instead of noisy review feeds.
* **🌐 Cross-Platform:** Accessible via web and native mobile clients.
